# Quick start (5 minutes)

From the site to the Word file, no theory.

## 1. Sign in

Open [nx.geostru.ai/atlante](https://nx.geostru.ai/atlante/?lang=en). If you already have a
GeoStru account, sign in; otherwise **Create your free account**: name, e-mail, phone, password,
profession, and one click on the link you receive by mail. Login is automatic and the same
account opens every NX app.

## 2. Point to the site

On the **New dossier** page:

- search an address or a municipality in the top field, or **click on the map**;
- drag the marker to the exact spot: the search proposes an indicative position;
- check **latitude and longitude** (WGS84) and the **radius** (metres) of the surroundings.

The buttons top right switch between **Map** and **Satellite**.

!!! tip "Perimeter"
    If you know the plot, draw it with **Perimeter → Draw** (or import a GeoJSON/KML): the
    sources are queried over the whole area, not only at the point.

## 3. Choose the profile and describe the assignment

The **report profile** (geological, geotechnical, seismic, structural, hydraulic, agronomic)
decides which sources are queried. Title, client and project description go into the Word file
and into the assistant's prompt: the more you say about the works, the more relevant the text.

## 4. Create the dossier

**Create the dossier**: the run takes from a few seconds to a couple of minutes and continues
even if you close the page. The page refreshes by itself; at the end you see the four counters
(*found*, *nothing at the point*, *unavailable*, *plates*) and the evidence by theme.

## 5. Read, complete, download

- **Generate the text**: the assistant writes summary and sections from the evidence only, with
  [n] on every sentence and the list of what remains to be checked.
- **Your surveys**: upload a PDF and accept the proposals you need. Then **Regenerate the text**
  to include them.
- **Download Word**: the document shaped like a report, with table of contents and chapters to complete.
- **Continue with the NX apps**: each card opens an app with the site already filled in.

!!! warning "An empty outcome is not a green light"
    "Nothing at the point" means the source answered and, at the scale of its mapping, nothing
    is reported. It does not prove absence of hazard or constraints: scale, edition and
    applicability to the plot remain to be checked.

The dossier stays in **My dossiers** (rename, rerun, delete) and can be saved to GeoDropbox with
the Word file and the `.atlante` file.
