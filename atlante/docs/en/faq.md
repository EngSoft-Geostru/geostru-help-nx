# FAQ

## How much does it cost?

Atlante NX is **free**: you only need a GeoStru account, created in a minute with e-mail
confirmation. No credits are spent on dossiers, plates, Word files or perimeters. The assistant
(text and PDF analysis) is covered by a free monthly allowance per user. The calculation apps you
continue to (Loadcap, Slope, …) run on credits: on registration you receive thirty welcome credits.

## Why do so many items say "nothing at the point"?

Because the source answered and, at the scale of its mapping, nothing is reported at the point:
no PAI perimeters, no IFFI landslides, no capable faults. That is information, and the dossier
writes it with due caution. It is **not** proof of absence of hazard or constraints: scale,
edition and applicability to the plot remain to be checked, and the Word file lists that among
the checks to complete.

## A source is "unavailable". What now?

The body's service did not answer within the time limit (it happens: services go down without
notice). The dossier completes anyway and the Word file lists the datum among those to acquire.
**Rerun** the dossier from *My dossiers* later: previous dossiers are kept.

## Can I trust the text?

It is written from the evidence only, with a [n] reference on every sentence, and validated on
return (non-existent references removed, sections without sources discarded). But it is a
**draft**: read and revise it as you would a colleague's. The dossier and the Word file say so.

## My survey PDF is not read correctly

The model reports only what is written; complex tables, low-quality scans or ambiguous units can
produce imprecise proposals. That is why every proposal carries **page and quotation**: check on
the document and tick only what is correct. Nothing enters the dossier without your selection.

## Where do my PDFs go?

They stay in your dossier, on the app server, visible only to you; they are sent to the model
that analyses them and nowhere else, and deleted with **Remove** (or with the dossier, after 12 months).

## Why does the Word file have empty chapters?

Because they are yours: "Conclusions" and the subsoil model do not derive from public sources.
The Word file is shaped like a report so that you find the chapters in the right place, not to
fill them in for you.

## Does it work outside Italy?

Not today: the sources are the Italian national ones. Extensions to other countries are planned.

## How many dossiers can I make?

Twenty per day per account and three in progress at once; they are kept for twelve months. If
you need more, write to [info@geostru.ai](mailto:info@geostru.ai).
