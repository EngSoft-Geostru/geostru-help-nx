# Access and credits

## Access

Hydrogeo NX runs in the browser: sign in at [nx.geostru.ai/hydrogeo](https://nx.geostru.ai/hydrogeo/)
with your **GeoStru NX account**. There is nothing to install. Every user can access all
stations.

## What is free

- Opening the app and the map.
- Searching for stations and seeing them on the map (messages to the assistant, however, use
  credits).
- Opening the **sheet** of a station with source, position, years of data and relevance.

## What uses credits

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Unlock data** of a station (includes the first table that opens) | 30 |
| Opening a table: Short Series, Long Series or Precipitation | 10 |
| CSV, Excel, PLV or XML export | 5 |
| PDF Report export | 10 |
| Message to the assistant | 3 |
| Basin contributions (through the assistant) | 12 |
| Importing a station into Runoff Lab NX | 10 |

Values as of 12/11/2026. Current tariffs are on the [Pricing](https://geostru.ai/en/pricing/)
page; your balance is in the user menu, where you can also top up.

## How long an unlock lasts

An unlock stays active **in the browser tab** where you made it. If you close the tab, or open
the app in another browser or on another device, the station is shown as locked again.

!!! warning "Tip"
    Before unlocking, choose the station carefully from its sheet (relevance, period, data
    types) and, once the tables are open, **export** what you need: the file is yours to keep.

## When you are not charged

- **An operation that returns no data is not charged**: for example the table of maxima of a
  station with daily rainfall only, or a Runoff Lab NX import with no series.
- **The same operation repeated shortly afterwards** (within the same 15-minute window) is not
  charged again: for example the same export downloaded twice.

## Not enough credits

If your balance is not enough, the app opens the top-up window with the credits required and
those available. No credit is deducted until the operation succeeds.
