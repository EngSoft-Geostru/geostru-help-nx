# Glossary

**Rainfall depth (h)**
:   Rainfall fallen over a given time interval, in millimetres.

**Duration (d, t)**
:   The consecutive time interval over which rainfall is measured: 1, 3, 6, 12, 24 hours, or
    1 to 5 days.

**Annual maximum**
:   The highest rainfall depth recorded in a year for a given duration. Series of annual
    maxima are the basis of the statistical analysis of heavy rainfall.

**Short series, long series**
:   In Hydrogeo NX, the annual maxima for durations up to 24 hours (short series) and from 1
    to 5 days (long series).

**Hydrological Annals**
:   Yearly publications of the Italian Hydrographic Service, now collected by ISPRA, with the
    rainfall, temperature and discharge records of Italian stations.

**Rain gauge, recording rain gauge**
:   A rain gauge measures accumulated rainfall, usually daily; a recording rain gauge
    (pluviograph) records it over time and gives the maxima over short durations.

**Relevance**
:   In Hydrogeo NX, the rating of the record length: good from 20 years, medium from 10 to
    20, low under 10.

**Return period (T)**
:   Average interval, in years, between two events that reach or exceed a given value. It
    comes from the statistical analysis, in Runoff Lab NX.

**Rainfall depth-duration-frequency curve, IDF curve**
:   Relation between rainfall depth and duration for a given return period, in the form
    h = a·tⁿ. In Italian practice it is called *curva di possibilità pluviometrica* (CPP).

**Gumbel, GEV, Pearson III, TCEV**
:   Probability distributions used for annual rainfall maxima. TCEV (Two-Component Extreme
    Value) is the model of the Italian VAPI project for regional analysis. They are in
    Runoff Lab NX.

**Hydraulic invariance**
:   The principle that developing an area must not increase the stormwater discharge sent
    downstream. Attenuation works are sized in HID NX.

**PLV**
:   GeoStru text format for exchanging rainfall data between programs.
