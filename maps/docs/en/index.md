# Maps NX

**Maps NX** covers the step that comes before the calculation: turning a place on
the map into coordinates, elevations and a drawing your CAD can open.

[**Open the app**](https://nx.geostru.ai/maps/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

---

## What it does

- **Find the site** — search by place name or address, with road, satellite and
  terrain base maps, switchable at any time without losing your drawing.
- **Draw** points, tracks and areas, with vertices editable by dragging. Every
  vertex lands in the coordinate table with its id, latitude, longitude,
  elevation and chainage.
- **Sample elevations** from the digital terrain model, on demand or
  automatically as you place each point.
- **Derive sections** — the profile of the points you clicked, or a section
  resampled at a regular step along the drawn track.
- **Generate grids** of elevation nodes over the drawn area.
- **Export** to TXT, CSV, LLE, NEZ (UTM), DXF, GMT and the section profile.

## Who it's for

Geologists and geotechnical engineers, surveyors, infrastructure designers,
hydrology and hydraulics technicians, environmental consultants, public-works
offices. In short: anyone who today keeps a web map open in one window, copies
coordinates into a spreadsheet in another, and hand-builds the file the CAD needs
in a third.

## An honest note about the elevations

!!! warning "Elevations come from a terrain model, not from a survey"
    Maps NX reads elevations from a digital terrain model of global coverage,
    typically on a grid of about **30 metres**. They are fine for feasibility and
    preliminary design; they **do not replace a topographic survey**. The page
    [Elevations and terrain model](quote.md) explains exactly what to expect and
    how to spot a value you should not trust.
