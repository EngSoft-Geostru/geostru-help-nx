# Quickstart — your first section in 5 minutes

In 5 minutes you'll see GeoSection NX at work on a real example.

## 1. Open the app

Go to [`nx.geostru.ai/geosection/`](https://nx.geostru.ai/geosection/).

## 2. Load an example

Toolbar → **Open example** → pick a packaged dataset (real geotechnical
boreholes with typical stratigraphy).

The app loads:

- **Map** with the borehole locations.
- **Section trace** already drawn.
- **Boreholes** (3 to 6 typical holes).

## 3. Inspect the boreholes

Click a borehole on the map → the side panel opens with:

- **Layer stratigraphy** (top, bottom, lithology, symbology).
- **Water-table depth**.
- **Core photos** (when available).
- **In-situ tests** (SPT, CPT, …).

## 4. Generate the section

Click **Generate section**. GeoSection:

1. Projects boreholes onto the trace (cumulative position along the polyline).
2. Draws the **stratigraphic columns** of the boreholes.
3. **Linear interpolation** between adjacent boreholes for horizons.
4. Draws the **water table** as a blue line (dashed in transition zones).
5. Generates the **soil table** at the bottom.

## 5. Export

Toolbar → **Export**:

- **SVG** — vector for Inkscape, Illustrator, web.
- **DXF** — vector for AutoCAD/BricsCAD.
- **PDF** — paginated report ready for delivery.
