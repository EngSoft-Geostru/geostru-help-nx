# Glossary

**MS3** — level 3 Seismic Microzonation: a study that provides, for the zones of the municipal territory, response spectra from numerical analyses.

**Elastic response spectrum** — maximum acceleration Se(T) of an oscillator with period T and 5 % damping.

**Regularised spectrum** — the MS3 spectrum brought back to the four-branch shape of NTC with Appendix 1 of Ordinance 55.

**Simplified approach** — the definition of the seismic action with the subsoil and topographic categories of NTC 2018 (§3.2.2–3.2.3).

**T~min~, T~max~** — the smallest and the largest of the main vibration periods of the building.

**T~R~** — return period; 475 years for the comparison of the Ordinance.

**a~g~, F~0~, T~c~*** — acceleration on rigid ground, maximum amplification and period at the start of the constant-velocity branch of the spectrum on rock.

**S~S~, S~T~, C~C~** — stratigraphic, topographic and period amplification coefficients.

**T~B~, T~C~, T~D~** — periods at the start of the plateau, of the 1/T branch and of the 1/T² branch.

**Pseudo-velocity** — SV = SA·T/2π.

**RSL** — local seismic response analysis (1D or 2D).
