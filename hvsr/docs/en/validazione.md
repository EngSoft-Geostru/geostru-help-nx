# Validation and limits

Point 10.2 of the Italian NTC 2018 asks the designer to assess the reliability of the code. This page summarises the material. The framework of GeoStru validations is described on [geostru.ai](https://geostru.ai/en/validation/).

## Fields of use

**Single-station ambient noise** measurements with a three-component sensor. 1D model of horizontal layers, compared with the fundamental mode.

## Limits to assess

- The model is **1D**: buried valleys, dipping layers, cavities and topography are not represented.
- A wave field from **diffuse sources** is assumed: nearby machinery, wind and poor coupling give non-stratigraphic peaks.
- The misfit uses the **fundamental mode only** and compares **shape**: the measured amplitude does not constrain the contrast.
- The inversion is **not unique**: multiplying H and V~s~ by the same number leaves the curve unchanged. Three models with the same curve give categories E, C and B. **Independent data are needed**.
- A **peak on the edge** of the band is not a peak, and f~0~ is the absolute maximum: with two similar peaks it can jump from one to the other.
- Below **20 minutes** the measurement is outside the SESAME recommendation.
- ρ and ν are assumed. Cases S1 and S2 are not covered, nor is joint inversion with MASW.

## Test cases

**Closed forms.** Resonance V~s~/4H: 20 m at 200 m/s give 2.5 Hz; the ellipticity maximum is 2.50 Hz with contrast 10, 2.65 Hz with contrast 6, 3.20 Hz with contrast 4. V~s,eq~: 6 m at 180 and 14 m at
320 m/s on bedrock give 20 / 0.07708 = 259.46 m/s, category E.

**Synthetic inversion.** A curve generated by a known model is found again: H = 14.000 m, V~s~ = 180.00 and 320.00 m/s, misfit 7.6·10⁻¹⁰. It is the most favourable case, without noise.

**Comparison with Easy HVSR desktop.** Seven recordings and nine projects: H/V curve, σ, spectra and maps agree within 10⁻⁹ relative, f~0~ to 12 decimals, the nine SESAME results exactly, ellipticity
within 10⁻⁹, misfit to 12 decimals. The reference is today's desktop program, not the numbers saved in 2011-2015 projects. This shows the port did not change the calculation, not that the method is right:
for that, the closed forms and the literature count.

## Differences from the desktop program

| Desktop | HVSR NX |
|---|---|
| STA/LTA filter that, on finding a window to discard at a certain point, also deleted the following ones: `Example_01` 0 windows, `Esempio_02` 16 | every window is checked: 30 and 44 |
| Averages over all windows or over the included ones, depending on the command | always over the included ones |
| V~s,eq~ not extended to 30 m and with the half-space thickness: 140.43 m/s, category D, on 5 m at 100, 5 m at 200, 300 m/s | infinite half-space: 211.76 m/s, category C |
| V~s,eq~ not computed with the first layer ≥ 800 m/s | V~s~ of the layer, category A |
| Foundation depth lost along the way; category from ground level | saved; V~s,eq~ and category from the foundation level |
| Labels of the two triangular smoothings swapped | correct labels |
| Search that could deliver a model worse than the start | the best is kept |
| Non-numeric readings silently set to 0 | explicit error |

Left as in the desktop program: 59 windows instead of 60 on 1200 s, the relative σ in the confidence band and the ratio of means as the curve.
