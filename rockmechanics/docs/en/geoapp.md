# Geoapp — online tools

**Geoapp** is GeoStru's web suite for online calculations: over 40 applications for engineering, geology, geophysics, hydrology and hydraulics. Most are free; some require a monthly or annual subscription.

## Why subscribe

A subscription lets you:

- use the professional applications anywhere and on any device;
- save files to the cloud and to your own PC;
- reopen files for later processing;
- print reports and graphical outputs;
- be notified of new applications and have them automatically included in your subscription;
- always have the latest versions;
- access support via tickets.

## Applications useful with Rock Mechanics

Among the Geoapp applications, several pair well with the Rock Mechanics NX workflow:

- Planar sliding along a plane
- 3D wedges
- Bolting (passive bars)
- Anchors (tiebacks)
- Anchored mesh systems
- Rigid and elastic rockfall barriers
- Stability analysis of planar surfaces

[Go to Geoapp](https://geoapp.geostru.eu){ .md-button }

!!! tip "Design of remedial measures"
    For two-joint kinematics and for sizing the remedial measures (bolts, anchors, meshes) on planar failure you can also use [RockPlane NX](https://help.nx.geostru.ai/rockplane/en/).
