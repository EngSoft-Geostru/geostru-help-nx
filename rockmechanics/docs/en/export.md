# Export and report

Once the classifications and verifications are complete, Rock Mechanics NX gathers all the work into a calculation documentation ready to be attached to the study. There are two outputs: the **calculation report** and the **graphical drawings**.

## Calculation report

The geomechanical calculation report is the main document produced by the app, in **Word** format. It presents the entire calculation process in an organized way, exactly as set up in the project:

- the **geostructural survey data** — discontinuity sets with the ISRM parameters (orientation, spacing, persistence, aperture, roughness, weathering, infilling, hydraulic conditions), in addition to $S_u$ and RQD;
- the **adopted classifications**, with index, class and quality of the rock mass for each method applied;
- the **characteristic parameters** obtained ($c$, $\varphi$, $E$, GSI), useful for the subsequent analyses;
- the **stability verifications**, with the input data, the results and the outcome.

The report is generated from the **Report / Report** section of the app: once the survey, classifications and verifications are complete, you produce the document and download it ready to be reviewed and signed.

!!! note "Multiple classifications in the same report"
    If you have processed the rock mass with several methods, the report presents them side by side: the scatter of the characteristic parameters is itself information about the reliability of the result.

## Graphical drawings

Alongside the report, Rock Mechanics NX produces the **graphical drawings** that accompany the text and make it easier to read:

- **projections and survey diagrams** — the representation of the discontinuity sets and their orientations;
- **verification schemes** — the representation of the kinematisms analyzed in the stability verifications.

The graphical drawings visually document both the survey and the verifications, and fit naturally into the technical report.
