# RPD NX

**RPD NX** is the web application in the GeoStru suite for the **design and verification of road pavements** — both flexible and rigid structures. It runs in the browser, with no installation, and pairs well-established calculation methods with a **real-time 2D/3D preview** and an **AI assistant** that explains every result.

[**Open the RPD NX app**](https://nx.geostru.ai/rpd/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Who it is for

Civil engineers, road designers and geotechnical engineers who need to **size or verify** the pavement package (wearing course, binder, base, subbase, subgrade) according to the reference methods of professional practice.

## What you can do

- Verify a pavement with three methods: **empirical AASHTO 1993**, **rational Ivanov** (deflection) and **rational Westergaard** (rigid pavements).
- Define geometry and layering while the **dimensioned 2D cross-section** and the **3D model** update as you type.
- Compute the **design traffic** in equivalent standard axles (ESAL of 8.2 t).
- Add a **bituminous geogrid** reinforcement and assess its contribution through the *Traffic Benefit Ratio* (TBR).
- Read results **explained by the AI** and, if the check is not satisfied, apply **actionable suggestions** with one click.
- Generate the **calculation report** (Word/PDF) and export the cross-section.

!!! tip "Where to start"
    If it is your first time, follow the **[Quick start (5 minutes)](quickstart.md)**: from the first login to reading the results.
