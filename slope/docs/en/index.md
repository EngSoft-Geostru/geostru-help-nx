# Slope NX

**Slope NX** is the GeoStru web software for **slope stability analysis** with the **limit equilibrium method** (LEM). It runs in the browser, with no installation, and pairs established calculation methods with a **real-time 2D/3D preview**, **verification to NTC/EC** with partial factors, and an **AI assistant** that explains every result and suggests remedial measures.

[**Open the Slope NX app**](https://nx.geostru.ai/slope/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Who it's for

Geotechnical engineers, civil engineers and geologists who need to **verify the stability** of a natural slope, an embankment or an excavation, with circular or general slip surfaces, in the presence of a water table, loads, seismic action and retaining structures.

## What you can do

- Verify a slope with five method-of-slices methods: **Fellenius**, **Bishop simplified**, **Janbu**, **Spencer** and **Morgenstern-Price** (GLE).
- Search the critical surface over a **grid of centres** (circular) or define a **general surface** by points, with automatic search.
- Model the ground in **layers** with geotechnical parameters (γ, γ<sub>sat</sub>, c′, φ′, c<sub>u</sub>), the **water table** and **distributed loads**.
- Analyse in **drained** (effective stress) or **undrained** (total stress) conditions.
- Verify to **NTC 2018**, **EC7-EC8** or user coefficients, with the partial factors of groups **M** (materials) and **R** (resistance).
- Include the pseudo-static **seismic action** (k<sub>h</sub>, k<sub>v</sub>), computed from NTC or imported.
- Add **retaining structures** — RC walls and gabions — with the **backfill** modeled as soil in the calculation.
- Read results **explained by the AI** and, if the check fails, apply **actionable measures** (e.g. drainage) with one click.
- Generate the **calculation report** (Word) and export the section; view the extruded **3D model**.
